Hermes Control Center

A locally operated content management application for organizing, reviewing and planning short-form videos for the Ardında Kalan media project.

What the application does

Hermes helps the authorized channel operator organize video projects, review assets and prepare platform-specific publishing plans. Platform connections use account-holder authorization when supported.

TikTok integration

The planned integration is intended to let the authorized account holder connect their TikTok account and submit their own video content through TikTok's approved APIs, subject to the permissions and review requirements granted by TikTok.

Current status: TikTok integration is under development and is not yet approved or enabled for live publishing.

Access and security

The operational dashboard is hosted locally and is not exposed through this informational website. This website does not request TikTok passwords, API secrets or access tokens.
