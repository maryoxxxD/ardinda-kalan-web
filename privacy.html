Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This policy describes the intended handling of information by Hermes Control Center, a locally operated content-management application for the Ardında Kalan media workflow. TikTok integration is under development. This policy must be reviewed and updated before live integration is activated.

Information involved

If an authorized operator connects a supported platform, the application may process account identifiers, account display names, authorization tokens, video files, captions, publication schedules, and publication status. It does not require the operator's platform password.

Purpose and use

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare and manage content, submit approved publishing actions, display status, and diagnose errors. Content is not posted without an action authorized by the account operator.

Storage and access

The application is designed to store operational records locally. Platform credentials and tokens are intended to be encrypted at rest and restricted to the local application operator. This public informational website does not receive or display those credentials.

Sharing

Information necessary to carry out an authorized platform action may be transmitted to that platform's official API. We do not intend to sell account or content information to advertisers.

Retention and deletion

The operator may disconnect platform accounts and request removal of associated local credentials and records. Some records may be retained as needed for security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations. Data already transmitted to a platform remains subject to that platform's policies.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are intended to protect stored credentials and limit access. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions, data access, or deletion requests, contact the application operator through the verified contact method published with the final production application. A contact address must be added before submission for platform review.

Changes

This policy may be revised when functionality or legal requirements change. The updated effective date will appear here.
